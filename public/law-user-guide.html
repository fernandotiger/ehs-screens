The big picture

A legal profile describes one site’s activities. It selects potentially applicable compliance checklists from a content library. You decide relevance, describe controls, evaluate evidence and follow up any gaps.

1. Define profile→2. Check relevance→3. Evaluate evidence→4. Follow up gaps→5. Review changes

Company structure and registers

Business unit → Site → Location → Department

Cork’s example is Operations → Cork Distribution → Warehouse → Logistics. Law uses its own Legal profile selector. When assigning evaluation work, the department choices belong to that profile’s site.

A register or domain here means Health & Safety or Environment; it is a subject area, not a website address. Other company requirements have their own register.

04

Your first practice run

Use a fictional workplace transport example at Cork. Perform every step as Alex. In a real team, Sam coordinates the evaluation and a task owner verifies the controls on site.

About 15 minutesCork · distribution complianceCL-001 · Workplace transport controls

Start from the sample data before installing the quarterly update. If you have already changed it, your numbers may differ. Reload only when you deliberately want to discard the app tab’s changes.

1

Choose Cork and open the topic

On Law → Overview, select Cork · distribution compliance from Legal profile. Open Compliance checklists, search “transport” and open Workplace transport controls. Read Requirements.

What you should see: The sample topic has a 45% self-assessment. Cork has two relevant checklists and one excluded checklist.
2

See the connected evidence

Open Risk & Incident. Open RA-1042 · Forklift movement in warehouse. Read the risk, then use its Legal compliance connection to return to this checklist. Open INC-261 · Near miss at loading bay and return the same way.

What you should see: Law opens the exact Risk or Incident record. Its return link opens Cork’s source checklist.
3

Complete the sample Law task

Open the checklist’s Tasks tab, then LT-001 · Verify pedestrian route controls across shifts. In Completion comment, enter a fictitious practice result: “Demo exercise: checked both shifts; route markings are visible, but supervision at loading bay 2 needs follow-up.” Click Complete task.

In real use, do the work before recording completion. This wording is only a presentation example.

What you should see: The task is Completed and keeps your comment. The checklist rating and evaluation are unchanged.
4

Record an evaluation

Click Open source checklist, then Evaluation. Keep Relevant to this profile checked. Review or extend Why relevant / controls & responsibility. In Evaluation of compliance / evidence, add: “Demo review of the task result and near-miss record: controls are documented; the loading bay supervision gap remains.”

Select Sam O’Brien as owner and Logistics as department, set a future Next evaluation review date, and click Save evaluation. Leave the sample score unchanged for this exercise.

What you should see: Both narratives, owner, department and review date are saved. Complete means the evaluation is recorded; it does not mean every gap is resolved or that the rating is 100%.
5

Read the change history

Open History to see evaluation changes. Return to Overview and inspect the overdue task count. Open Reports → Compliance register for a tabular view.

What you should see: The task no longer contributes to the open overdue count. The self-assessment stays 45% unless you deliberately change it.
Next practice: install the sample update and accept it for Cork. The changed workplace transport evaluation needs an explicit content review. For a new compliance gap, create a draft incident or corrective action.

06

Create and maintain a profile

Suggested person: Alex sets it up; Sam owns the Cork questionnaire and keeps it current. A profile describes what potentially applies to its site, rather than granting access to that site.

1

Create the profile

Open Law → Legal profiles → Create profile. Enter a name, choose an existing owner and site, and add a useful description. This presentation catalogue supports Ireland.

Example: “Dublin · maintenance activities”, Alex as owner, Dublin Plant as site.

What you should see: The profile has a named owner and one site. Create the company structure in Configuration first if the site is missing.
2

Describe the activities

Under Activities, materials & equipment, select the activities that actually describe the site: Workplace operations, Workplace transport, Machinery, Chemicals, Waste or Air emissions. Choose the workspace sharing / rating options and click Save profile & generate scope.

What you should see: Matching checklists enter the profile. A new profile uses the latest installed content version; each evaluation still needs to be completed.
3

Keep the questionnaire current

On its profile card use Edit profile when operations change. The existing profile’s site is fixed; use a new profile for a different site. Save the revised activity questionnaire.

What you should see: Newly matched topics become visible. A removed activity leaves its saved evaluation outside current scope; it does not erase that record.

Profile card controls

ControlMeaning
Open registerSelect this profile and open its checklists.
Share with workspace / Make privateDemonstrate the intended visibility arrangement. This admin-only prototype still allows Alex to operate every profile.
Hide / Show compliance ratingHide or show the self-assessment presentation without deleting saved ratings.
Archive profile / Restore profileKeep a profile for history instead of deleting it. Restore it before continuing normal checklist edits.
Two ways to remove a topic from today’s working view. Change activities when the site’s operations have changed. Use Not relevant with a reason when an individual generated checklist does not apply. Both preserve previous evaluation records.
07

Record relevance and evaluation

Suggested people: Sam coordinates the Cork register; Nora evaluates Dublin topics; Liam checks the supporting evidence. There is no separate Law approval screen in this prototype.

1

Read the requirement

Open a checklist’s Requirements tab. Read the requirement summary and associated source entries before recording your own findings.

What you should see: You understand which topic you are evaluating and which source content it belongs to.
2

Explain applicability and controls

On Evaluation, keep Relevant to this profile checked if it applies. Fill Why relevant / controls & responsibility: explain where it applies, what controls are in place and who maintains them.

Example: “Forklifts operate in the Cork warehouse. Marked routes and traffic rules are maintained by Logistics.”

What you should see: The first narrative explains the context and controls, rather than just saying “yes”.
3

Evaluate evidence and assign responsibility

Fill Evaluation of compliance / evidence: describe what was checked, what it showed and any remaining gaps. Choose an existing Responsible owner, a department from the profile’s site and a future Next evaluation review date. Add evidence references in the next tab.

What you should see: The evaluation has a traceable basis and someone responsible for keeping it current.
4

Save progress or the completed evaluation

Optionally use the Self-assessed compliance rating slider; Clear rating leaves it unassessed. Click Save evaluation. You can save partial notes and return later.

What you should see: Complete requires both narratives, owner, department and next review date, with no pending content-review flag. The score is optional and is a separate measure.

When a checklist is not relevant

  1. Uncheck Relevant to this profile.
  2. Enter a specific Reason this is not relevant, explaining the decision.
  3. Save. Find it later using the Not relevant filter.
  4. Use Mark relevant again if the situation changes, then review the saved evaluation.

Exclusion preserves notes, ratings, evidence, tasks and links. Excluded items do not contribute to the relevant checklist denominator.

Outstanding work needs its own record. A written gap in the evaluation does not automatically create a task or incident. Add the follow-up explicitly in Tasks or Risk & Incident.
08

Keep evidence and company requirements

Suggested person: the evaluator maintains the evidence; task owners supply the supporting records.

Evidence on a checklist

  1. Open Evidence and add file references or a safe HTTP(S) document link using the evidence controls.
  2. Save the evidence using the tab’s save control.
  3. Describe the actual findings in Evaluation; a file reference alone does not complete the evaluation.

Examples: “Cork route inspection.pdf”, a training register or a maintenance inspection record. This demo retains names and links only. The Document module is still a placeholder, so there is no working controlled-document upload or approval flow here.

Add an other requirement

1

Describe the commitment

Open Law → Other requirements → Add requirement. Enter Requirement name, category, Requirement / commitment and optional comma-separated keywords.

Example: “Corporate contractor induction standard” — “Confirm induction and authorisation before work begins; retain the signed induction record.”

What you should see: The commitment belongs to the selected profile rather than the shared legislation catalogue.
2

Evaluate it like the other topics

Click Save requirement. Its checklist opens. Record relevance, controls, evaluation, owner, department and next review; add evidence, tasks or cross-module links as needed.

What you should see: The company commitment uses the same checklist tabs and contributes to the relevant evaluation view when active and relevant.

Use its list controls to edit or deactivate a commitment when appropriate. Keep history and previous evidence rather than treating deactivation as proof that the commitment was satisfied.

09

Assign and finish Law tasks

A Law task is a piece of work linked to a checklist. A corrective action created in Incident includes a separate effectiveness process. Choose the record that fits the work.

1

Create and assign the task

From a checklist’s Tasks tab add a task, or use Law → Tasks → Create task. Enter Task description, source checklist, responsible person, responsible department, target closure date, priority and additional information. Click Save task.

What you should see: The task has a deadline, an owner and a source checklist. The department must belong to the profile’s site.
2

Find and do the work

Search the Tasks page and use owner, status and priority filters. Open a task to read the additional information. Use Edit task to change an open task, or Open source checklist for context.

What you should see: The owner understands both the work and the compliance topic it supports.
3

Record completion

After doing the work, enter Completion comment, explaining what was done and the outcome. Click Complete task. A blank comment blocks completion.

What you should see: The completed task retains its comment and history. Its completion does not automatically change the checklist rating or clear a content-review flag.

Archive task / Restore task keeps the record and history. Archive is different from completion: hiding a task does not prove the work was done. The demo’s daily checks and email reminders are not connected to these live Law records.

10

Connect Risk and Incident

Legal evaluations can use Risk assessments and Incident records as supporting evidence or follow-up. The modules keep their own decisions: a link does not automatically approve a risk, close an incident or change compliance.

Link an existing record

  1. Open a checklist’s Risk & Incident tab.
  2. Under Link an existing record, choose Risk assessment, Incident or Corrective / preventive action.
  3. Choose a record in this profile’s site.
  4. Add a useful Hazard, aspect or link context and click Link record.
  5. Open the connected record. Its Legal compliance panel returns to the exact profile checklist.

Ordinary associations can be unlinked without deleting the record. A record created from Law retains its origin; its origin link is protected.

Create a compliance incident

1

Create the draft from Law

Under Address a compliance gap, choose Department for new follow-up, then click Create compliance incident draft.

What you should see: A new Non Compliance incident appears in the connected list with its Law source. It is deliberately a Draft.
2

Complete and submit in Incident

Open the generated incident. Edit its report, check the type, event details and assignments, answer required template questions, and submit through the normal Incident flow. Follow the Incident guide for reporting and investigation.

What you should see: The published Incident report template and its version are preserved. Required answers must be supplied; creating a Law draft does not submit or close it.
Where forms come from. Sofia drafts an Incident / Report form in Configuration → Form Builder; Alex publishes it and binds it to Non Compliance in Incident Settings. Without a compatible published form, draft creation is blocked with an explanation. Law checklists themselves come from the legal content library and profile questionnaire.

Create a corrective action instead

  1. Choose the follow-up department and click Create corrective action.
  2. Enter the action description, owner and due date.
  3. Click Save corrective action, then open its connected record.
  4. In Incident → Actions, carry out the work and any child tasks, record completion and plan the effectiveness review.
  5. The assigned reviewer checks effectiveness at the appropriate time. See the Incident action guide.

A Law-origin corrective action can exist without a linked incident because its source is the compliance checklist. Follow-up actions keep that legal origin. The evaluator must separately revisit Law when the evidence changes.

11

Install content and review changes

Suggested people: Alex installs the shared content package; Sam accepts it for the Cork profile; the evaluator then checks affected controls and evidence. These are three distinct steps.

1. Preview & install library→2. Accept per profile→3. Review affected evaluations
1

Preview the sample package

Open Law → Changes & updates → Content library. Click Preview sample quarterly update to inspect the 2026.Q4 package. Alternatively use Download sample JSON, then the upload field. Review the New / Amended rows before installing.

What you should see: The preview includes amended workplace transport content and a new emissions topic. Previewing alone changes nothing.
2

Install the library version

Click Install content into library.

What you should see: The installed library advances from 2026.Q3 to 2026.Q4. Existing profiles still use their own accepted versions; customer evaluations are preserved.
3

Accept for Cork

Select Cork · distribution compliance. Open Profile updates, compare the profile version with the installed library, read the changes, and click Accept update into this profile.

What you should see: Cork uses 2026.Q4. Dublin remains at 2026.Q3 until accepted separately. Only matching activities add checklists: Cork does not gain the sample emissions checklist because it has no Air emissions activity.
4

Re-evaluate the amended transport topic

Under Evaluations affected by accepted content, open Workplace transport controls. Read the current Requirements and content history. On Evaluation, update the controls, findings, evidence and review date as needed.

After checking them, tick I have reviewed controls and evidence against the current requirements and click Save evaluation. The completed evaluation fields must also be present.

What you should see: The content-review flag clears through this explicit review. Existing notes, evidence, tasks and Risk / Incident links remain available.
Installation does not equal acceptance, and acceptance does not equal compliance. Changed associated legislation can require review even if the checklist text itself is unchanged. Unaffected evaluations keep their existing status. An overdue review date still needs attention after a content review.

Use History to distinguish customer evaluation changes from requirement content history. JSON uploads are previewed and validated, with a 2 MB limit. This is a sample manual workflow, not a connected provider feed or an automatic quarterly delivery service.

Understand statuses and dashboard numbers

What you seeWhat it means
Relevant checklistsActive, currently scoped topics not excluded with a reason. Other active company requirements are included.
Evaluations completeBoth narratives are recorded, with no pending content-review flag. This measures recording progress, not legal compliance.
Self-assessed complianceAverage of saved scores on relevant, in-scope topics only. The rated-count coverage tells you how many contributed. Unscored topics are not treated as 100%.
Not assessedNo optional rating has been recorded. It can coexist with a completed written evaluation.
Overdue tasksOpen active Law tasks with a target date before today. Completed or archived tasks are excluded.
Reviews needing attentionTopics flagged for content review or past their next evaluation date. Hiding ratings changes the Overview emphasis, while preserving scores.

Checklist status

StatusYour next move
Not startedStart the applicability / controls and evidence narratives.
PartialFinish the missing narrative and required responsibility / review details.
CompleteKeep the evidence current; follow up any recorded gaps and review at the next date.
Review requiredRead changed requirements, update evidence and explicitly confirm the content review.
Not relevantKeep a defensible reason; mark relevant again if circumstances change.

Sample Cork starts at 45% from one rated topic out of two relevant topics; the unassessed waste topic is not included in the average. Dublin starts at 80% from three rated topics out of five. Score colours are red below 50, amber 50–79 and green 80–100; they visualise the user’s self-assessment rather than certify compliance.

Questions and practice checklist

Why do Dublin and Cork show different topics?

Each profile uses its own site, activity questionnaire, accepted content version and exclusions. A library update can be accepted for one profile while another keeps the old version.

Why is there no update to accept?

The initial profiles already use the latest installed library, 2026.Q3. Preview and install the sample 2026.Q4 package in Content library first, then accept it separately for the selected profile.

Why is an evaluation complete while its score is low?

Complete means the evaluation is recorded. Its evidence may describe gaps. The optional score measures a separate self-assessment; neither completion nor a high rating establishes legal compliance.

Why did finishing a task leave the score and review flag unchanged?

Task completion records work. The evaluator must review the new evidence and make an explicit evaluation decision. A content-review flag also requires its confirmation checkbox.

Why can’t I save a completed evaluation?

Check both narratives, an existing owner, a department in this profile’s site and a valid next review date. A Not relevant decision requires a reason. Partial notes can be saved before completing the evaluation.

Why does an excluded topic seem to have disappeared?

The normal working filter shows relevant topics. Switch to Not relevant or the appropriate wider relevance view. An activity removal appears under Outside current profile. Saved notes, evidence and links are retained.

Why can’t I find a record to link?

The selection is limited to this profile’s site and the chosen record type. Check the selected profile and the record’s department/site. Linking does not create broader access rights.

Why is my new incident still a draft?

Law creates a Non Compliance draft with the published Incident report template. Complete its mandatory answers and event details, then submit in Incident. Do not create another draft just because the first is not submitted.

Who approves a Law evaluation?

This prototype saves an evaluation and supports explicit content review; it has no separate approval stage. Liam’s independent assurance role is a proposed operating process. Incident corrective action effectiveness has its own reviewer step.

Why did my changes vanish after refresh?

App records are stored only in that tab’s memory. Navigation preserves them; reload and sign-out reset them. The guide’s practice ticks are saved separately in browser storage when available.

Do the fictitious roles control access now?

No. Use only the admin login for this presentation. The responsibility table helps design future scoped roles; confidentiality and permission enforcement remain production work.
