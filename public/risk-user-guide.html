Assessments tell you which work matters

A risk assessment records an activity, what could go wrong, the controls already in place and the remaining risk. It can contain several hazards or environmental aspects.

Assessment→Hazard / aspect→Improvement task→New score & history

An assessment task can also belong to a management programme. This connects a specific hazard to the organisation's wider objectives.

Safety example

Activity: forklifts moving pallets.
Hazard: moving vehicles.
Consequence: a pedestrian could be injured.
Current control: marked walkways.
Improvement task: install a physical barrier.

Environmental example

Activity: transferring solvent.
Aspect: a release to water.
Impact: water contamination.
Current control: bunded storage.
Improvement task: improve containment checks.

Where does a record belong?

Northstar→Operations→Cork Distribution→Warehouse→Logistics

Company → business unit → site → location → department. Choosing a department on an assessment identifies its location, site and business unit automatically.

Roles and scope work together

A role describes what a person can do. Its scope describes where they can do it. For example, Nora could be a Risk Assessor at Dublin Plant and a Risk Reader at Cork Distribution. That would let her edit Dublin assessments and only read Cork assessments.

In Configuration → Roles & permissions, use the Roles tab to define actions and the Assignments tab to give a user a role at a company, business unit, site, location or department scope. Some responsibilities in this guide, such as programme management or periodic review, still need separate permission design in the future application. Choosing someone as an owner does not itself grant them a role.

Prepare a template

For Alex and Sofia. Every new assessment starts from a published Risk form.

You can skip this chapter on your first attempt. The demo already includes published Health & Safety multi-hazard, Environment multi-aspect and Health & Safety FMEA forms.
Configuration → Form Builder → Create form
  1. Enter a name, for example Contractor activity assessment.
  2. Choose the Risk module and 5x5 scoring method. Click Start building.
  3. Select the Risk domain: Health & Safety or Environment.
  4. The builder supplies the key module fields. Add company-specific questions from Components, such as a short-text Permit reference field or a dropdown Work shift with Day / Night options.
  5. Drag a component onto the canvas, or click Add. Select it to change its label, help text, required state or options.
  6. Use Reuse a created field to search field names and copy a custom field from another saved form to the end of this form.
  7. Reorder fields by dragging or using the up/down buttons. Preview the design, then click Publish version.
  8. Open Risk → Risk assessments → Create assessment. Your published form appears in the template choices.

A draft form does not appear as an assessment template. Publishing makes it available for use in this session.

Where do the questions appear?

The assessment workflow supplies the core scope, ownership, scoring and review controls. Your custom form questions appear under Template questions in the first assessment step. Saved custom answers appear on the assessment scorecard. Required custom answers must be supplied before submission or approval.

Reusing a field makes a separate copy. Editing the copy does not change its source. A new assessment keeps the template version and rules selected when it was created, so later template edits do not silently change existing records.

08

Understand the score

A score helps prioritise work. Always read the justification and controls too.

3×3 and 5×5 matrices

Likelihood describes how likely the event is. Severity describes how serious its consequence is.

Likelihood × severity = score

In a 5×5 example, 4 × 5 = 20. The template's thresholds determine whether that score is Low, Medium, High or Critical.

FMEA

A more detailed form groups factors into severity, likelihood and difficulty of detection.

RPN = average severity × average likelihood × average detection difficulty. The prototype rounds the result. Factors use 1–10; a higher detection value means it is harder to detect the problem in advance.

Checklist scoring

For each item, answer whether its controls are satisfactory. No gives an action-required finding; Yes and Not applicable do not. The prototype displays 1 for action required and 0 for satisfactory. These are findings indicators, not matrix risk scores.

FMEABelow 5050–249250–499500–1,000

The overall assessment score is its highest individual hazard/aspect score. If one hazard scores 20 and another 6, the overall score is 20. Different scoring methods have different scales and should not be ranked directly against one another.

Configure the rules

Risk → Risk matrix
  1. Select the Risk template you want to configure.
  2. Set the Medium, High and Critical starting thresholds. They must increase in that order and fit the method's range.
  3. Choose a default review frequency and the number of days to notify before a review.
  4. Choose the sites allowed to use the template. No selected sites means all sites.
  5. Add a footnote and click Save scoring rules.

Saving rules for a published form creates a new template version. New assessments use it; existing assessments retain their original rules.

Change the method and domain in Form Builder. A matrix image upload is a reference preview; it does not set numeric rules and is not retained after leaving the page. Advance-reminder settings do not send notifications in this prototype.

Create the objective

Risk → Objectives → Create objective

Title: Improve warehouse pedestrian safety. Choose Health & Safety, Logistics, a responsible person and a deadline. Describe the desired outcome: “Reduce shared-route exposure through effective segregation.” Save.

The objective describes the outcome. The target below makes it measurable.

2

Create a measurable target

Risk → Targets → Create target

Title: Complete vehicle-route upgrades. Link the new objective and assign an owner. Choose Increase, unit %, baseline 0, current 0 and goal 100. Add the deadline and save.

For a decreasing measure, such as resource consumption, choose Decrease and a goal below the baseline.

3

Create the management programme

Risk → Management programmes → Create programme

Title: Pedestrian route improvement project. Link the objective and tick Complete vehicle-route upgrades under Linked targets. Choose Logistics, an owner, start date and deadline. Record the scope and resources, for example “Plan, install and inspect route segregation.” Save.

A programme must link to at least one target under its selected objective. Its deadline cannot be earlier than its start date.

4

Add the work

Create an MP task from the programme for general project work, or select this programme when creating an RA task from an assessment hazard. See the next chapter.

5

Record measured progress

When measurements show that half the routes have been upgraded, open the target and click Edit / update measurement. Change Current value to 50. Keep the baseline and goal unchanged, then save.

The target shows 50% progress towards the goal. Programme progress separately shows completed parent tasks.

Two progress bars, two meanings. A programme's percentage is the proportion of completed parent tasks. A target's percentage is progress from its baseline towards its goal. Example: baseline 60%, current 82%, goal 100% means 55% of the improvement has been achieved, even though the current measured value is 82%.

Programme status is edited explicitly, and the prototype blocks setting it to Completed while linked tasks remain open. A target can only be marked Completed when its recorded current value reaches its goal. Objectives are marked Completed explicitly; review their targets and programmes first.

10

Create and complete tasks

For Nora or Liam to organise; Priya to carry out the assigned work.

RA · RISK ASSESSMENT TASK

Improve a specific assessed risk

Open an approved assessment. On its Scorecard tab, click Add improvement task beside the hazard/aspect.

  1. Enter the task title and useful instructions.
  2. Choose a scoring factor, such as Likelihood, and a target factor score.
  3. Select a management programme and responsible person.
  4. Set creation and target completion dates.
  5. Click Save task.

The target score is the selected factor's value, not the whole assessment score. “Follow-up only (no rescoring)” records work without changing a score.

MP · MANAGEMENT PROGRAMME TASK

Deliver general programme work

Open a programme and click Create MP task.

  1. Enter the task title and instructions.
  2. Confirm its programme and responsible person.
  3. Choose creation and target completion dates.
  4. Click Save task.

MP tasks support the programme without being tied to a hazard or changing an assessment score. Example: arrange supplier demonstrations or run an awareness campaign.

Break larger work into sub-tasks

Open a task and click Add sub-task. Enter the smaller piece of work, owner and dates. The sub-task retains its parent link. Every child must be completed before its parent can close. Sub-tasks do not rescore assessments.

Record completion

  1. Open Risk → Tasks and find the task. Read its instructions and related assessment/programme.
  2. Finish any open sub-tasks first.
  3. Click Complete task.
  4. Enter a completion date between creation and today, plus comments describing the work and evidence.
  5. For a scoring RA task, enter the Actual outcome factor value. It may differ from the target if the achieved outcome differs.
  6. Click Save completion.

A scoring RA task updates its hazard factor, recalculates the assessment and adds a revision. MP tasks, sub-tasks and follow-up-only tasks record completion without rescoring.

For a checklist factor, actual value 0 means satisfactory and 1 means action required. For FMEA, a task can change an individual named factor; the section averages and RPN are recalculated. The assessment must be Approved when completing a task that changes its score.

Completion is a recorded outcome. Selecting an actual score does not verify that physical controls were installed. A real team must check effectiveness before recording that outcome. The current prototype demonstrates the record flow; it does not have a separate task-verification stage.

Review an assessment and keep history

For the assessment owner and approver.

“It is still valid.”

Open an approved assessment and choose Record periodic review. Enter the review date, next review date and comments. Click Save review.

This records a check without changing the score or revision. You can read it under History & reviews.

“Something has changed.”

Choose Edit / rescore. Update the relevant scope, hazards, controls, scores or justification. Save a draft or submit it for approval, then approve the new content.

The revision increases when the edited assessment is approved. Earlier scorecard snapshots remain in history.

Use the detail tabs

  • Scorecard: hazards, controls, scores, justification, recommendations and custom answers.
  • Tasks: assessment tasks and sub-tasks, with links to open them.
  • Documents & links: related assessments, document names/reference URLs, attachment filenames and illustrative legal checklist topics.
  • History & reviews: earlier scorecards, task-driven revisions and periodic reviews. Click View saved scorecard to inspect a retained snapshot.

History currently retains scores and hazard/control snapshots rather than a full version of every header field. Document references do not upload document bytes; attachment controls retain filenames only. Legal checklist topics are sample content. Confidential marks a record visually and does not restrict access in this admin demo.

For the assessment owner, administrator and people working around the site.

A QR label identifies a particular Risk assessment. Attach it to the relevant equipment, such as Ladder LAD-001. Scanning opens that assessment after sign-in; an approved assessment opens its periodic review form. The QR does not approve the assessment or complete a review.

Alex prepares the label

  2. Open the record and click QR label.
  3. Check the equipment name, responsible person and current review date.
  4. Use Print or Download label to produce a label. Keep its white border clear and the code readable.
  5. Use Test QR link to try the route on this computer.

Priya reviews at the equipment

  1. Open Scan QR label from Workspace or the top-bar scan icon.
  2. Tap Start camera and allow camera access when prompted. Point at the printed code.
  3. Alternatively choose a QR image, or enter RA-LAD001 and click Open record.
  4. Read the approved assessment and check whether its controls are still valid.
  5. If unchanged, enter review comments and a future next review date, then Save review. If changed, use Edit / rescore and follow the approval workflow.
Two different records can describe one ladder. RA-LAD001 describes risks and controls; INS-LAD001 is a routine condition questionnaire. They have separate QR labels. Use the Inspection ladder walkthrough for the checks and next inspection date.

Try it in five minutes

  1. Open the bell icon Due-date reminders and choose Priya Shah under Responsible person.
  2. Find the ladder Risk review due today. Open it from the reminder, or type RA-LAD001 in the scanner.
  3. Enter a review comment such as “Controls checked against the current task; no changes required.” Choose a future review date and save.
  4. Reopen the bell. The old due-today reminder disappears when the new date is outside the configured upcoming window.
  5. Open History & reviews to read the review entry.

This fictional exercise demonstrates the software workflow. Review actual equipment using your organisation’s procedure and manufacturer instructions.

How due reminders work

The bell is a live in-app preview of enabled reminder rules. Risk reminders use the approved assessment’s owner and review date; Inspection reminders use the assigned inspector and scheduled date. Filter by responsible person to demonstrate the hand-off.

Use Manage reminder schedules or Configuration → Scheduled jobs. The default Daily equipment & risk reminders rule covers upcoming Risk reviews and Inspections within seven days. Overdue rules cover late items. Rules must be enabled, include the record type and matching company/site scope, and have owner reminders with in-app delivery selected to appear in the bell. Test previews use live Risk and Inspection dates; other module entries remain illustrative.

Phone and prototype limits. Set the label’s website address to the deployed HTTPS workspace before printing for a phone. A localhost link works only on this computer. A direct link requires sign-in; scanning does not grant access. Camera and QR-image decoding happen on the device. If the camera is unavailable, use an image or reference. Demo edits reset on reload/sign-out and are not shared between devices. Background cron execution and automatic email delivery still require the backend; this demo sends no messages.

Keep the label useful

  • Periodic reviews and revisions keep the assessment’s ID, so its existing QR still opens it.
  • Cloning creates a different assessment and needs a new label.
  • If the record is archived, scanning still identifies it but does not make it editable. Restore and follow reapproval as needed.
  • A missing record or a label from another workspace shows an error. Open the correct workspace or check the reference.

14

Questions, checklist and useful terms

Quick answers when you are unsure what to do next.

Why can't I see my form under Create assessment?

Open Configuration → Form Builder. Check that the form belongs to the Risk module and is Published, then return to Create assessment. Draft forms are not listed. Form changes exist only in the current app session.

Why won't the assessment submit or approve?

Check the required scope fields, owner, approver and review date. For every hazard/aspect, fill in the activity, hazard/aspect, consequence, current controls and scoring justification. Also answer required custom template questions. If the template restricts sites, select a department at one of those sites.

Why is Complete task disabled, or why does completion fail?

Finish every sub-task first. Use a valid completion date and supply completion comments. If the task changes a score, its assessment must currently be Approved. Reapprove a draft/awaiting-approval revision before completing that task.

Why did the overall score stay the same after a task?

The assessment displays its highest hazard/aspect score. You may have reduced one hazard while another remains the highest. A task marked Follow-up only, an MP task or a sub-task does not rescore. Also check the actual factor outcome recorded when the task was completed.

Why is a target still at 82% when its programme is at 100%?

Programme progress counts completed parent tasks. A target tracks a measured value entered by its owner. Open the target and use Edit / update measurement when you have new evidence. Programme status and objective status are also changed explicitly rather than automatically.

Will overdue records send emails in this demo?

No emails are sent. The bell previews live due reminders for Risk reviews and Inspections using enabled Scheduled jobs rules. Saving a new review date updates that preview. Background execution and email delivery require the backend. See QR labels and reminders.

How do I recover an archived assessment?

Open Risk assessments, set Status to Archived, open the record and choose Restore as draft. Update the content and approve it when ready.

Can Nora or Liam sign in as separate users now?

No. They are fictional learning personas. Use Alex's demo login to play all parts. User records and scoped role assignments can be demonstrated through Configuration, but Risk permission enforcement and separate-user sign-in are not implemented.

Why have all my changes disappeared?

The prototype keeps data in memory. Reloading the app or signing out resets it. Keep the app tab open during a presentation, and use this guide in a separate tab.

Small glossary

Hazard
Something with the potential to harm people, such as a moving vehicle.
Environmental aspect / impact
An aspect is how an activity interacts with the environment; an impact is the resulting change.
Current controls
Measures already in place to manage the risk being assessed.
Residual risk
The risk remaining with the current controls in place.
Scorecard / assessment
The record describing the activity, hazards/aspects, controls and ratings.
RA task / MP task
RA tasks improve a specific assessment. MP tasks support a programme independently of assessment scoring.
RPN
Risk Priority Number: the result of the FMEA calculation.
Revision / periodic review
A revision records changed approved content. A periodic review records a validity check.
Role / scope
A role defines allowed actions. Scope defines where that role applies.
Risk domain
The assessment subject: Health & Safety or Environment. This differs from a sign-in/SSO domain and is not a company-hierarchy level.
