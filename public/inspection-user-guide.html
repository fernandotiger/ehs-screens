02

The big picture

An inspection is a routine check of conditions at an area or item of equipment. A finding is a check marked Needs attention. A Risk assessment describes hazards, controls and scores. An audit checks a wider programme or process. Use the simple Inspection module for everyday checks.

1. Schedule→2. Observe→3. Respond→4. Complete checks→5. Track follow-up

Complete the fieldwork

Every check has an answer/result and the inspection has a closing summary. Any issue has a recorded response.

Resolve the problem

A linked corrective action may still be open after fieldwork completes. Carry out its work and verify effectiveness in Incident.

Business unit → Site → Location → Department

Selecting a department supplies its parent site. Example: Dublin Plant → Maintenance. Form visibility and related Risk assessment choices use that site.

06

Prepare the questionnaire

Sofia drafts forms; Alex publishes them in this suggested team arrangement.

  1. Open Configuration → Form Builder, or Open Form Builder from Inspection → Templates.
  2. Use Create form, enter a name and select module Inspection.
  3. Keep the supplied metadata fields. Add questions from Components or search for a previously created field and add it to the end. Reorder fields and enter their labels, options and guidance.
  4. For Yes / No checks, use positive wording: “Are walkways clear?” Yes means Pass; No means Needs attention. Use other field types where appropriate and choose explicit results during inspection.
  5. Configure required questions and site visibility, review the preview, then publish.
  6. Return to Inspection and schedule with the published form.
Version snapshots. A scheduled inspection retains its captured questionnaire. Editing/publishing a form affects new inspections. Repeat or next-date scheduling uses the current available published version. A draft-only or site-unavailable form cannot schedule a check.

Starter forms: Workplace inspection, Equipment pre-use check and Ladder condition check. Their questions are demonstration content.

07

Schedule or repeat an inspection

Sam coordinates the schedule; Priya or another named inspector carries out the check.

  1. Open Inspections → New inspection.
  2. Enter an identifiable title, department, inspector, published template, scheduled date and area/equipment.
  3. Add instructions describing what to check, then Schedule inspection.

Use Edit schedule to change the title, inspector, date, area or instructions without discarding recorded work. The department and captured template remain fixed. Keep the equipment identifier consistent when a physical QR label is already attached.

Same equipment, next check

Enter Next inspection date when completing, or use Repeat inspection. A fresh record retains the original QR reference and clears old observations/evidence. Next-date completion requires a future date and no other outstanding check using that label.

Different equipment

Use New inspection and print its own label. Repeating the old ladder record would keep the old ladder’s QR identity.

There is no automatic inspection recurrence here. Next-date completion creates one scheduled check now; Repeat opens the scheduling form. If several checks share a label, scanning opens the earliest active outstanding check.

08

Record checks and complete fieldwork

  1. Open the assigned record on Checklist. Set Actual inspection date to the day observed, today or earlier.
  2. Answer each question and choose its result: Pass, Needs attention or N/A.
  3. For N/A, explain why the check does not apply. For Needs attention, describe the issue and record a response.
  4. Use Save progress if interrupted. Navigate within the same app session to return to saved work.
  5. Add inspection evidence and a Closing summary. Optionally set Next inspection date.
  6. Click Complete inspection.
100% means results are recorded. Completion also checks required answers, valid dates, N/A explanations, issue responses, critical immediate responses and the closing summary. Read the displayed error to find what is missing.

A No answer on a Yes / No question cannot be marked Pass. Other question types need an explicit appropriate result. Completed fieldwork is read-only. Archiving preserves the record and findings; restore an archived incomplete check before recording more work.

10

Follow up across modules

The coordinator follows the issue; the action owner carries out work; the reviewer checks its effectiveness.

  1. Open Inspection → Findings. Filter by site, owner or status, and select a finding.
  2. Use Open action or Open incident on the original check.
  3. For an action, carry out its tasks/work, record completion evidence and request/record the effectiveness review in Incident. Effective work resolves the finding; ineffective work needs follow-up.
  4. For an incident, finish required report questions, submit the draft, then complete its investigation/actions and closure requirements.
  5. Use the source Inspection link in the Risk/Incident record to return to the exact inspection and highlighted check.
Completed inspection ≠ resolved finding. The inspection records what was observed. Its finding reads the live linked Incident status, owner and due date. Completing tasks alone does not prove effectiveness; creating an Incident draft alone does not submit or close it.

Once routed, the original observation is locked to preserve what was sent. Manage linked ownership, deadlines and progress in Incident. Finding evidence supports the observation; inspection-level evidence supports the overall visit. Evidence controls store filenames/descriptions/URLs only in this prototype.

If Incident creation is blocked, check that Incident → Settings has an active Inspection Finding type/subtype and a published report form selected. Required questions are completed in the generated draft, using Form Builder’s Incident template.

11

Print and scan equipment labels

Prepare

  1. Open an inspection and click QR label.
  2. Check the equipment identifier, inspector and due date.
  3. Set Label website address to your deployed HTTPS workspace for phone use.
  4. Print or Download label. Keep the clear white border around the code.
  5. Attach it to the correct equipment and test it.

Use in the field

  1. Open the app’s Workspace → Scan QR label or top-bar scan icon.
  2. Tap Start camera, allow camera access and scan the label.
  3. If needed, choose a QR image or enter its INS reference, then Open record.
  4. Sign in if opening the QR link directly while signed out.
  5. Complete the current questionnaire.

The label survives repeat checks. A repeat has its own inspection ID for history but keeps the original label reference. Scanning selects the earliest active unfinished check. With no unfinished check, it opens the most recent active completed record; use Repeat inspection to schedule the next one.

The ladder’s condition check INS-LAD001 and its safe-use Risk assessment RA-LAD001 have separate labels. Scanning the Risk code opens its approved periodic review, rather than this condition checklist.

Access and demonstration limits. A QR identifies a record; it does not grant access or automatically complete it. Camera/image decoding runs on the device. Phones need the deployed HTTPS address; localhost is only for this computer. New demo records are not shared across devices and disappear on reload/sign-out. Use Test QR link or the seeded ladder references for this presentation.
12

Understand due reminders

The bell displays a live preview of due work from enabled Scheduled jobs rules. Inspection reminders use the assigned inspector and scheduled date; Risk review reminders use the approved assessment’s owner and review date.

  1. Open Due-date reminders in the top bar.
  2. Choose Responsible person to view Priya’s or Sam’s items.
  3. Click a reminder to open the linked record.
  4. Use Manage reminder schedules to open Configuration → Scheduled jobs.

The default Daily equipment & risk reminders rule covers the next seven days. Overdue jobs match late records. For the bell, a rule must be enabled and match the target, scope, lead/overdue threshold, owner recipients and in-app channel. An email-only rule is not displayed there.

Completing an inspection removes its old due item. A newly scheduled check appears when its date enters the matching window. Saving a Risk review updates its due date. Job test previews use these live records; other module samples remain illustrative.

No messages are sent. This preview refreshes while the workspace is open. Running jobs in the background and sending automatic emails need production backend integration. Reminder rules do not create recurring inspections.
13

Read the overview and reports

Use the overview for a quick team conversation: which checks still need work, which are overdue, which findings remain open, and which fieldwork is completed? A completed fieldwork count does not mean all related corrective actions are closed.

  1. Open Reports to see the Inspection register and Finding register.
  2. Apply search/site/status filters relevant to your meeting.
  3. Use Export inspections or Export findings for a spreadsheet. Use your browser’s Print command to print the page or save it as PDF.
  4. Open the original record for its answers, evidence references and history.

Archived inspection findings remain traceable. Give readers a suitable site scope in the proposed role model. The current admin demo allows all records to be read.

14

Questions and practice checklist

Why is my form missing when scheduling?

It must belong to Inspection, be Published, and have questions visible for the department’s site. Scheduled checks retain their snapshot; new publication affects new schedules.

Why will Complete inspection not work?

Read the displayed message. Check actual date, every result and required answer, N/A reasons, issue responses, critical immediate response and closing summary. Create a selected action/incident before completing.

Why did my QR open an older check?

It opens the earliest active unfinished check using that label. Complete or archive outstanding old checks as appropriate. If none remain, it opens the latest active completed record.

Why can’t I set the next date?

It must be a future valid date. A current published form must be available for the same site, and no other active unfinished check can already share the label.

Why can’t my phone open the label or start the camera?

Use the deployed HTTPS workspace address. A localhost label points to the device itself. Allow camera access when prompted; try a QR image or type the reference if camera access is unavailable.

Why is a completed inspection’s finding still open?

Its Incident corrective action needs effectiveness verification, or its Incident draft needs submission and closure. Inspection completion only finishes fieldwork.

Can Priya or Sam sign in separately?

Use admin@northstar.example for this presentation. Names explain proposed responsibilities; separate-user login and scoped permissions are not enforced.

Why did changes disappear?

App data is in memory. Reload or sign-out restores the seed data. This guide’s practice ticks use separate browser storage when available.
